ParallaxCompare 0.0.20 adds a side selector to Find and speeds up comparisons and merges of files with many repeated lines.

- **Find in either file.** Choose Left, Both, or Right in the text view. Highlights, the match count, and navigation follow the chosen side. Switching sides stays near the current match. If searches overlap, only the current search can move the selection or reveal hidden rows. (#38, #41)
- **Faster diffs and merges.** Updated the diff engine to fix quadratic preprocessing on files with many repeated lines. This speeds up text comparisons and opening merges with thousands of conflicts. (#40)
- **Folder view.** Closed folders show their file counts on the folder row. (#39)
